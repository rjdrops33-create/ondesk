What the desk handles

Answers live, every time

A conversational voice picks up on the first ring — not a voicemail box, not a menu maze. It talks like a person because it is built to.

Screens the call

Asks the questions you always ask: name, job, address, timeline. Hot jobs get flagged to your phone instantly. Tire-kickers get handled politely.

Texts and messages

Follows up on iMessage and WhatsApp so the person who called still gets an answer while you are with a customer. Same desk, same voice, every channel.

Books and reminds

Confirms the time, texts a reminder the morning of, and reschedules without phone tag. Fewer no-shows, zero phone tag.

The book

Logs who called, what they wanted, and the next step. You get a short recap you can read in ten seconds — plus the full call transcript whenever you want it.

Outbound follow-up

Need the quote chased or the appointment confirmed? The desk picks up the phone and calls out — politely, persistently, and in your shop’s voice.

Gets you paid

Sends a payment link by text the moment the job is booked. The desk collects deposits — it never promises a price you didn’t set.

Looks it up mid-call

Customer asks about your hours, your services, your service area? The desk answers from your shop’s own sheet — and can research anything it doesn’t know yet.

Never sleeps badly

After hours, on a job, mid-holiday — the desk keeps the same energy. One AI, one voice, no sick days, no payroll.
